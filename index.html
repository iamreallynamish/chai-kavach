How it's made

From waste to worth in five steps.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Used tea leaves from homes and chai stalls, marigolds from temples and twigs from pruned neem trees — never from healthy trees that are cut down.

  2. 2

    Dry

    Everything is washed of milk and sugar residue and sun-dried completely, so nothing rots or grows mould.

  3. 3

    Grind & sieve

    The dried tea, marigold petals and neem leaves are ground and sieved into a fine powder.

  4. 4

    Shape

    For sticks and dhoop, the powder is mixed with a natural binder into a dough and rolled onto neem twigs or shaped into cones. For pouches, dried leaves are filled into raw jute bags.

  5. 5

    Cure & pack

    Products are dried again until fully cured, then packed in paper and jute — no plastic.

How to use

Simple instructions and safety precautions for each product.

How to use

  1. Hold the bare neem end and light the coated tip.
  2. Let it flame for a few seconds, then gently blow it out so it smoulders.
  3. Place it upright in a holder or a bowl of sand, near doors or windows.
  4. Let it burn out fully; collect the ash once cool.

⚠ Safety

  • Never leave a burning stick unattended.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains and paper.
  • Use in a ventilated room; avoid if you have asthma or smoke sensitivity.
  • Not for eating. Store in a cool, dry place.

How to use

  1. Place one cone on a heat-proof dish or dhoop stand.
  2. Light the pointed tip and let it catch for a few seconds.
  3. Blow out the flame; the cone will smoulder and release fragrant smoke.
  4. One cone lasts roughly 15–20 minutes.

⚠ Safety

  • The cone base gets hot — always use a heat-proof surface.
  • Never leave it burning unattended or overnight.
  • Keep away from children, pets and flammable items.
  • Ventilate the room; stop use if you feel discomfort.

How to use

  1. Place a pouch in shoes, the fridge, cupboards or drawers.
  2. It absorbs odour and moisture naturally.
  3. Every 2–3 weeks, refresh it by keeping it in sunlight for a few hours.
  4. After about 2 months, empty the tea leaves into plant soil as compost and reuse the jute bag.

⚠ Safety

  • Not for eating — keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • In the fridge, keep it away from direct contact with uncovered food.
  • Do not let it get wet; replace if it gets damp or smells musty.

Responsible claims: our pilot tests compared batches for burning time and visible smoke. We say our sticks "help keep mosquitoes away" — they are not a medical product. Laboratory testing is our next step before large-scale production.

FAQs

Are the products really made from used tea leaves?

Yes. Used tea leaves are collected, cleaned of milk and sugar, sun-dried and ground. They are the main ingredient in all three products.

Do Kavach Sticks kill mosquitoes?

No. They contain no insecticide. The neem and marigold smoke helps keep mosquitoes away. We're planning lab testing before scaling up.

Does the pouch make my fridge or shoes smell of tea?

Only a faint, earthy smell at first. Dried tea leaves absorb odours rather than adding fragrance.

How long does each product last?

A stick burns for about 30–45 minutes, a dhoop cone for about 15–20 minutes, and a pouch stays effective for about 2 months if you sun it regularly.

Is the packaging plastic-free?

Yes. Sticks and dhoop come in paper packs and the pouch itself is raw jute.

Can I donate used tea leaves?

Yes, please! Dry them first and get in touch through the contact details below.

Do you take bulk or event orders?

Yes — use the order form and mention the quantity you need.
