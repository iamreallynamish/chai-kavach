How it's made

From waste to worth.
In five steps.

01
🫖

Collect

Used tea leaves from homes and chai stalls, marigolds from temples, twigs from pruned neem trees — never from cutting healthy ones.

02
☀️

Dry

Washed of milk and sugar, then sun-dried completely so nothing rots or grows mould.

03
⚙️

Grind & sieve

Tea, marigold petals and neem leaves are ground and sieved into a fine powder.

04
🤲

Shape

Mixed with a natural binder and rolled onto neem twigs or shaped into cones. For pouches, dried leaves fill raw jute bags.

05
📦

Cure & pack

Dried again until fully cured, then packed in paper and jute. Zero plastic.

How to use

Simple to use.
Safe by design.

How to use

  1. Hold the bare neem end and light the coated tip.
  2. After a few seconds, gently blow out the flame so it smoulders.
  3. Stand it in a holder or a bowl of sand near doors or windows.
  4. Let it burn out fully. Collect the ash once cool.

Safety

  • Never leave a burning stick unattended.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains and paper.
  • Use in a ventilated room. Avoid if you have asthma or smoke sensitivity.
  • Not edible. Store in a cool, dry place.

How to use

  1. Place one cone on a heat-proof dish or dhoop stand.
  2. Light the pointed tip and let it catch.
  3. Blow out the flame. The cone smoulders and releases fragrant smoke.
  4. One cone lasts roughly 15–20 minutes.

Safety

  • The base gets hot — always use a heat-proof surface.
  • Never leave burning unattended or overnight.
  • Keep away from children, pets and flammable items.
  • Ventilate the room. Stop if you feel discomfort.

How to use

  1. Place in shoes, the fridge, cupboards or drawers.
  2. It absorbs odour and moisture naturally.
  3. Every 2–3 weeks, refresh it in sunlight for a few hours.
  4. After ~2 months, compost the leaves in plant soil and reuse the jute bag.

Safety

  • Not edible — keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • In the fridge, avoid direct contact with uncovered food.
  • Keep it dry. Replace if it gets damp or smells musty.

Responsible claims: our pilot tests compared batches for burning time and visible smoke. Kavach Sticks “help keep mosquitoes away” — they contain no insecticide and are not a medical product. Laboratory testing is our next step before large-scale production.

    Questions? Answers.

    Are the products really made from used tea leaves?

    Yes. Used tea leaves are collected, cleaned of milk and sugar, sun-dried and ground. They're the main ingredient in all three products.

    Do Kavach Sticks kill mosquitoes?

    No. They contain no insecticide. The neem and marigold smoke helps keep mosquitoes away. Lab testing is planned before we scale up.

    Will the pouch make things smell of tea?

    Only a faint, earthy smell at first. Dried tea leaves absorb odours rather than adding fragrance.

    How long does each product last?

    A stick burns for about 30–45 minutes, a dhoop cone about 15–20 minutes, and a pouch stays effective for about 2 months if you sun it regularly.

    Is the packaging plastic-free?

    Yes. Sticks and dhoop come in paper packs, and the pouch itself is raw jute.

    Can I donate used tea leaves?

    Please do! Dry them first and get in touch using the details below.

    Do you take bulk or event orders?

    Yes — use the order form and mention the quantity you need.
