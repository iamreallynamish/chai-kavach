The story behind your pack

India drinks a lot of chai — and almost all the used tea leaves end up in the bin, along with temple flowers and pruned neem branches. We're four students — Yahana, Bhavika, Saavi and Meera — who wondered: what if yesterday's chai could protect us today?

Your product was made by collecting that waste, drying it, grinding it into powder and shaping it by hand. No synthetic fragrance. No plastic.

Kavach Sticks

  1. Light the coated tip, holding the bare neem end.
  2. Blow out the flame after a few seconds so it smoulders.
  3. Stand it in a holder or sand near doors and windows.
  4. Let it burn out fully. Put cool ash into plant soil.

Safety

  • Never leave burning unattended.
  • Keep away from children, pets and anything flammable.
  • Use in a ventilated space.
  • Not edible.

Kavach Dhoop

  1. Place one cone on a heat-proof dish.
  2. Light the tip, then blow out the flame.
  3. Enjoy the fragrance for about 15–20 minutes.

Safety

  • The base gets hot — use a heat-proof surface.
  • Never leave burning unattended or overnight.
  • Keep away from children and pets.
  • Ventilate the room.

Kavach Pouch

  1. Place in shoes, the fridge, cupboards or drawers.
  2. Sun it for a few hours every 2–3 weeks to refresh.
  3. After ~2 months, compost the leaves and reuse the jute bag.

Safety

  • Not edible — keep away from children and pets.
  • Keep it dry. Replace if it gets damp.
  • In the fridge, avoid direct contact with uncovered food.
